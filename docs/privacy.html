Privacy Policy

SocialPoster · Last updated: October 12, 2026

SocialPoster is a desktop application that runs on the user's own Windows computer. It helps the user publish images and videos they choose to their own social media accounts.

1. Data we access

When you connect your YouTube account, SocialPoster requests the youtube.upload permission only. It uses this permission solely to upload videos that you select, with the title and description you write, to your own YouTube channel. SocialPoster does not read, modify or delete any other data in your Google or YouTube account.

2. Where your data is stored

Access tokens, settings and your publishing history are stored only on your own computer (in the %APPDATA%\SocialPoster folder). They are never sent to the developer or to any third-party server. Your media files are sent only to the platforms you choose to publish to.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone. The developer has no access to your data.

4. Google and YouTube

SocialPoster uses YouTube API Services. By using it to upload to YouTube, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. SocialPoster's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking access

You can disconnect YouTube at any time from the app's Settings page ("Disconnect"), and revoke access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the %APPDATA%\SocialPoster folder removes all locally stored data.
